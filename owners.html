How it works

From your home to guaranteed rent

A transparent process that takes the work off your plate from day one.

Share your property

Fill in a two-minute form with your home's location, size and current status. It costs nothing and there's no obligation.

Free assessment & rent estimate

Our team assesses your property — remotely or with a visit — and gives you a fixed monthly rent figure based on location, condition and demand.

Sign a simple agreement

Once you're happy with the terms, we sign a clear digital agreement. No fine print, no brokerage — and it works fully online if you're overseas.

We furnish & upgrade

At our own cost, we furnish and style your home with furniture, appliances and a modular kitchen — turning it into a premium, rent-ready property.

We place & manage tenants

We market the home, screen tenants, sign their agreements and handle everything through the tenancy — including all maintenance.

You get paid, every month

Your fixed rent lands in your account on the same date each month — guaranteed, whether or not the home is occupied.
